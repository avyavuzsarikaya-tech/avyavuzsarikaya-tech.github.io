    Wheat, maize, and rice cross borders as balance sheets before they cross them as ships.

    Wheat, maize, and rice do not travel as headlines. They travel as contracts, port drafts, and seasonal windows. The public agencies that watch those flows publish balance sheets: production, trade, and stocks left at the end of the year, country by country.
